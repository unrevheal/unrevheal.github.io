Bio

Unrevheal are a metal-hardcore band formed in Trieste in 2018. They blend metalcore, hardcore, nu metal and grunge into a powerful, visceral sound driven by fast-paced rhythms, sharp riffs and high-energy vocals. Their music explores human fragility and emotional struggle, transforming rage, pain and vulnerability into an intense, direct and deeply personal experience.
